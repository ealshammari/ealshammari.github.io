About

A scholar who
writes in verse

Faculty at Kuwait University's College of Life Sciences, I hold a PhD from George Mason University. My academic publications appear with Springer and Taylor & Francis, spanning cybersecurity, Arabic NLP, and Islamic literary studies.

As a poet, I compose by musical ear and instinct — rooted in the deep classical Arabic tradition, particularly Gulf poetry and prophetic praise verse.

Research

Areas of Inquiry

01
Cybersecurity Behavior Modeling
Agent-based simulation of anti-phishing behavior using Protection Motivation Theory (PMT-X). Survey instruments and maturity assessment for real-world security decisions.
02
Arabic NLP & Gulf Manuscripts
Natural language processing for Arabic text. Visual knowledge structures in 19th-century Gulf manuscripts held at Kuwait's Ministry of Islamic Affairs.
03
Quranic & Classical Arabic Studies
Lexical cartography of Surat Yusuf. Prophetic praise poetry in Kuwaiti literature. Classical Islamic commentary and Arabic prosody.
04
Gulf Heritage & Children's Literature
Bilingual book series preserving Gulf cultural memory. Arabian Gulf manuscript documentation and historical cartography.
