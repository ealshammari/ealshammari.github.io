About

A scholar who
writes in verse

Faculty member at Kuwait University's College of Life Sciences, my work spans the arc between computational research and classical literary heritage. I hold a PhD from George Mason University and have published with Springer and Taylor & Francis.

My research navigates cybersecurity behavior modeling, Arabic natural language processing, and data science — while my creative soul dwells in the rhythms of classical Arabic prosody, Gulf manuscript culture, and the living tradition of Arabic poetry.

I compose by musical ear and instinct, rooted in the classical meters yet free in their expression.

Research

Areas of Inquiry

🛡

Cybersecurity Behavior

Agent-based modeling of phishing behavior using Protection Motivation Theory (PMT). Survey instruments, maturity assessment, and behavioral simulation targeting real-world security decisions.

🌙

Islam in China

Islam–Confucian synthesis in Chinese Islamic thought. Digital counter-memory, demolished mosques, and the survival of Islamic visibility. Han Kitab methodology and the scholarship of Wang Daiyu and Liu Zhi.

📜

Arabic NLP & Gulf Manuscripts

Natural language processing for Arabic text. Visual knowledge structures in 19th-century Gulf manuscripts. Manuscript access at Kuwait's Ministry of Islamic Affairs Department of Manuscripts.

🏺

Quranic & Classical Studies

Lexical cartography of Surat Yusuf. Connections between Quranic figures and ancient Egyptian history. Classical Islamic commentary and cross-linguistic literary analysis.

🗺

Historical Cartography

Ptolemaic maps of the Arabian Peninsula. Historical documentation of Kuwait and the Gulf region. Archive research including the Gertrude Bell Archive.

📚

Children's Literature

Bilingual children's book series on Gulf heritage (تراث خليجي). Stories that carry cultural memory to younger generations in both Arabic and English.
